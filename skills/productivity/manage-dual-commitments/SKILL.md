# Workflow: Balancing Dual Commitments

When the user is juggling multiple high-pressure deadlines (e.g., student work scholarships and internship assessments), follow this protocol:

1. **Information Extraction**:
   - Immediately offer to parse task files (PPTs, PDFs, Docs) related to assessments.
   - Use `vision_analyze` or `read_file` to understand requirements and technical stack.
2. **Timeline Management**:
   - Create a realistic 7-day schedule (or as per deadline).
   - Prioritize tasks based on complexity and energy levels (using user's "Pertamax Turbo" / energy status).
3. **Well-being Check**:
   - Incorporate rest intervals, acknowledging the importance of the user's support system (e.g., family advice on sleep).
4. **Active Assistance**:
   - Provide summaries, plan drafting, or technical research as requested to reduce cognitive load.

**Verification**:
- Confirm the schedule with the user.
- Break down complex assessments into small, actionable steps.
- Offer regular progress reminders.
